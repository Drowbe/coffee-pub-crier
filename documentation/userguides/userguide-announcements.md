# Combat and Round Announcements

**Audience:** GMs and players at the table.

The cards that mark the shape of a fight rather than a single turn: combat beginning, each new round,
and combat ending.

![The combat start and round announcement cards, reading Combat Begins and Round 1](../assets/crier-combat-turn.webp)

## When they post

- **Combat start** posts when the fight actually starts, not when you first build the encounter. A
  tracker you are still filling in stays silent.
- **A round card** posts at the top of each round.
- **Combat end** posts when the combat is ended and cleared.

Only one client posts each of these -- the GM -- so nobody sees a doubled announcement, and deleting
a fight you never started announces nothing.

## Why the first ones wait

Round announcements hold until every combatant has rolled initiative, for the same reason turn cards
do: the order is not final until the last roll lands. On a table that rolls one at a time, expect the
round card to appear at the end of the rolling rather than the start.

## Choose which ones you get

Each is an independent choice, under its own settings section:

- **Combat Cards** -- *Do Not Announce Combat*, *Announce Start Only*, *Announce End Only*, or
  *Announce Start and End*.
- **Round Cards** -- *Do Not Announce Rounds* or *Announce Rounds*.

![The Combat Configuration settings: Combat Cards, Start Label, End Label, Card Icon, Card Theme, Combat Start Sound and Combat End Sound](../assets/crier-settings-combat.webp)

## Change what they say

**Start Label** and **End Label** are plain text -- type whatever the table should read.

**Round Label** defaults to `Round {round}`. The `{round}` is replaced with the round number; keep it
where you want the number to fall, or remove it and the card carries only your text.

![The Round Configuration settings: Round Cards, Round Label, Card Icon, Card Theme and Round Start Sound](../assets/crier-settings-round.webp)

Their colour and icon are set the same way as every other card -- see
[appearance](userguide-appearance.md) -- and their sounds are in [sounds](userguide-sounds.md).
